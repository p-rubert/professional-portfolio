Innovoyce Fiber Cell Layout Improvement

A team Kaizen project focused on improving the fiber-cell and gowning-area layout to address process motion, contamination potential, and workflow efficiency.

KaizenCell layoutProcess flowParticulate testing

Project Overview

The Kaizen report documents a team review and improvement of the Innovoyce fiber cell and gowning-area layout. The work focused on reducing unnecessary motion, improving workflow, and reducing potential contamination sources.

Problem Statement

The report identified layout inefficiencies in the fiber cell and gowning area as contributors to increased contamination potential and reduced throughput. It also called out wasted motion during fiber assembly, excessive rework, and defects associated with contamination.

Project Objectives

  • Improve fiber-cell and gowning-area layout to streamline workflow.
  • Reduce unnecessary production motion and the waste identified by the team.
  • Reduce potential contamination sources and improve cleanliness controls.
  • Address rework and defect concerns affecting throughput.

Technologies Used

  • Kaizen report with current-state and improved-layout diagrams.
  • Particulate testing and before/after data comparison, as referenced in the report.
  • Air-conditioning installation and room-layout changes are referenced in the particulate comparison.
