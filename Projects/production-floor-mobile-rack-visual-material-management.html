Production Floor Mobile Rack & Visual Material Management Improvement

A collaborative operational improvement at Cogmedix in West Boylston, MA, using project-specific mobile racks, designated staging areas, signage, and color-coded floor markings to visually distinguish incoming raw materials from materials designated for rejection.

Inventory Control LeadMobile material racksVisual managementMaterial segregation

Operational Challenge

Production-floor material staging needed clear, visible distinctions between incoming raw materials and materials designated for rejection, while supporting project-specific material movement between warehouse and production.

Project-Specific Mobile Racks

Collaborated on implementing project-specific mobile material racks on the production floor. The mobile racks provide a physical means to stage and move materials in support of project workflows.

Incoming Material Staging

Incoming raw materials are staged in designated areas marked with blue-and-white striped floor tape. Signs identify this area using the exact label “Incoming Material Rack”.

Rejection Staging

Materials designated for rejection are placed in separate areas marked with red floor tape (red floor markings). Signs use the exact label “To be rejected”. This describes a physical staging distinction, not a formal quality disposition or approved quarantine system.

Visual Management Controls

The staging approach uses 5S-inspired visual management: project-specific mobile racks, clearly worded signs, blue-and-white striped markings for incoming raw materials, red markings for materials designated for rejection, and physical separation between the two staging areas.

The illustration is conceptual and does not depict an actual facility layout. The approach is not presented as a formal 5S certification or an audited compliance result.

My Collaborative Contributions

  • Collaborated on implementing project-specific mobile material racks on the production floor.
  • Supported the physical staging approach for incoming raw materials and materials designated for rejection.
  • Contributed to clear area identification through floor markings and signage.
  • Helped strengthen visual management, material segregation, and warehouse-to-production workflows.

Operational Value

Separate physical staging areas, visible floor markings, and clear labels help distinguish material staging purposes and support material segregation and warehouse-to-production workflows. No time savings, cost savings, quality metrics, or regulatory outcomes are claimed.
