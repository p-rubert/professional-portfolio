Project Overview

This completed collaborative implementation provides monthly reporting of production and warehouse inventory performance. I also participated in monthly operational performance review meetings, where the team reviewed KPI results, compared performance against targets, discussed operational risks and inventory discrepancies, evaluated potential root causes, and determined corrective actions and improvement priorities for the following month.

Business Challenge

Production and warehouse inventory measures need a consistent monthly view so teams can review actual performance against targets, observe changes over time, identify operational risks, and focus improvement discussions for the next month.

KPI Framework

The dashboard includes the following verified measures and reporting areas:

  • Cycle Count Accuracy.
  • Part-Bin (Location) Accuracy.
  • Fiscal Accuracy.
  • Quantity Adjustments.
  • Bin/Location Audit Accuracy.
  • Cycle Count by Part Class A/B/C/D.
  • Inventory adjustment root-cause categories.

Monthly Reporting

Monthly actuals and trend charts provide a recurring view of performance changes across production and warehouse inventory measures. Color-coded results make reported status and areas for attention easier to scan.

Target vs Actual Performance

Monthly actuals are reviewed in relation to quarterly targets to show target attainment and changes over time. This public case study does not reproduce actual values, target figures, or confidential employer performance data.

Monthly Operational Improvement Cycle

  1. MeasureReview monthly KPI results.
  2. EvaluateCompare actual performance against targets.
  3. InvestigateDiscuss performance gaps and evaluate potential root causes.
  4. PlanDetermine corrective actions and next-month priorities collaboratively.

Inventory Adjustment Root Causes

Quantity adjustments are considered alongside the dashboard’s inventory adjustment root-cause categories. This provides a structured reporting view for reviewing adjustment patterns without publishing specific operational records or unverified cause categories.

Operational Risk Identification

Changes in accuracy, audit, adjustment, and target-attainment measures help surface areas for operational review. The dashboard supports risk identification; it does not itself establish a cause or claim a measured risk reduction.

Next-Month Improvement Planning

Monthly trends, target attainment, color-coded status, and identified areas of concern inform discussion of focus areas for the following month. Improvement priorities are reviewed as part of ongoing operational planning.

My Collaborative Contributions

Operational Value

The monthly reporting approach gives teams a shared view of actuals, quarterly targets, trends, status, and areas requiring attention. It supports performance monitoring, operational risk identification, and next-month improvement planning. No measured improvements, savings, automated data integrations, or specific dashboard software features are claimed.
