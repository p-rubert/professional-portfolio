Technical project 02 / TeamDynamix

Enterprise Inventory Discrepancy Management System

Led implementation of a TeamDynamix ticketing process with IT to standardize inventory discrepancy reporting, support root cause investigations, and track corrective actions across more than 10 buildings.

Business Challenge

Inventory discrepancies originate across a multi-building operation. The business needed a consistent way to report those issues, investigate root causes, and keep corrective actions visible through follow-up.

Operational Scope

The implementation supports inventory discrepancy ticketing across more than 10 buildings. Each building submits approximately 500 tickets annually, representing an estimated 5,000+ tickets per year across the implementation.

The annual ticket volume is an estimate, not a verified exact count.

Leadership Contributions

  • Led implementation of the enterprise inventory discrepancy management process.
  • Connected inventory operations needs with IT during implementation.
  • Supported adoption of a shared approach across a distributed, multi-building operation.
  • Kept the process focused on report consistency, investigation, and corrective action follow-through.

Collaboration with IT

Partnered with IT to implement the TeamDynamix ticketing process for inventory discrepancies. The work brought operational reporting and technical implementation together around a consistent route for submitting and following up on issues.

Discrepancy-to-Action Workflow

  1. ReportSubmit an inventory discrepancy as a ticket using a standardized reporting approach.
  2. InvestigateReview the discrepancy and investigate its root cause with the relevant teams.
  3. Define actionIdentify a corrective action based on the investigation.
  4. Track follow-upRecord and track corrective action progress through the ticket process.
  5. Review patternsUse recurring issues and follow-up as inputs to ongoing process improvement.

Standardized Reporting

A shared ticket-based process gives buildings a consistent way to raise inventory discrepancies. Standardized reporting creates a common starting point for teams to review issues and determine the investigation needed.

Root Cause & Corrective Actions

Tickets support root cause investigations and provide a place to track corrective actions and follow-up. This connects the reported discrepancy to the work needed to address it, without assuming that every issue has the same cause or resolution.

Business Value & Continuous Improvement

The implementation establishes a shared structure for discrepancy intake and follow-through across buildings. It supports consistent reporting, investigation, and corrective action tracking so operational teams and IT can work from a common ticket process.

The ticket volume indicates the approximate operating scale, not a measured outcome. Reviewing recurring discrepancy themes and corrective action follow-up can inform continued process improvement. No time savings, cost savings, accuracy improvement, or other quantified result is claimed.
